Blowball Station

A dandelion seed head as a space station. A 6 m hub carries 12 pods, each made of 7 Spacelab-derived flight segments, and each pod ends in a sun-tracking solar bloom. The New Shuttle docks on the axial pod. It is assembled one pod per launch, in opposite pairs (the decussate-leaf / phyllotaxis rule).

The thesis: one blowball

Assembly, one launch at a time

The balance point returns to within 0.7 m of the hub after every second launch. The 0.15 m left at full bloom comes from the docking pod having no bloom.

Berthing: launch 6, seven segments, one at a time

The station arm lifts each segment out of the New Shuttle's 26.3 m bay, steps it 12 m clear, swings it round the outside of the station at a 59 m radius, then slides it in along the pod axis. Segments berth from the hub outward. Every path for launches 2–12 was checked offline and clears by at least 0.32 m.
