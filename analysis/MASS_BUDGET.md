### What this changes

- **The New Shuttle must lift ~42 t to station orbit, not 32 t.** That is about 2.6× the original Shuttle's ~16 t to ISS orbit. The vehicle needs Starship-class lift, or the pod has to be split across two flights.
- Launch 1 (hub + docking pod) is about 60 t: Starship-class.

## Berthing flange: bolts against cabin pressure

- With every hatch open, the pressure on a pod's end bulkhead passes through every segment joint:
  p·π·r² = 101.3 kPa × π × 2.03² = **1,312 kN**.
- An ISS CBM carries ≈258 kN across a ~1.8 m seal, with 16 bolts at ≈84.5 kN preload each.
- With CBM-class bolts, a full-diameter flange needs **24 bolts** to hold a bare 1.5× the pressure load in preload, or **82 bolts** to match the CBM's own margin (5.2× preload over pressure load). The model's 16 latches are not enough on their own.
- **Decided ([BERTHING_RING.md](BERTHING_RING.md)):** keep the pressure inside each segment behind its own bulkheads and seal only a Ø2.0 m passage, held by 20 bolts at the CBM's margin. The 16 latches capture and pull in; the bolts seal.
