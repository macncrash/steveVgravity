**Tightest anywhere: 0.12 m while lifting out of the bay, 0.28 m outside it.** In the bay the segment starts on its trunnions just above the floor and slides out between the sills, so small gaps there are by design; outside the bay is where the station's own growth matters. Final pose error is the largest of the centre offset (mm) and the axis angle (degrees) at the berth.
